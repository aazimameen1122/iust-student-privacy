Privacy Policy — IUST Student

App: IUST Student (com.mussey.iustapp) · Developer: Aazim Ameen · Effective date: 28 October 2026

IUST Student is an unofficial, student-developed companion app for students of the Islamic University of Science and Technology (IUST), Awantipora. It is not an official app of IUST. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information the app handles

  • Portal login credentials. If you choose to save your IUST portal login, your registration number and password are stored only on your device, in encrypted storage (Android EncryptedSharedPreferences, AES-256, with the key held in the Android Keystore). They are protected by your fingerprint or screen lock. The developer never receives, sees, or stores your credentials on any server.
  • Portal data. The app fetches your attendance, results, and notices from the official IUST portal using your own login, and stores copies (such as attendance history) on your device so the home-screen widget and history graph work. This data stays on your device.
  • No account with the developer. The app has no sign-up of its own, and the developer operates no server that collects your data.

How information is used

Your credentials are used only to log in to the official IUST portal on your behalf, inside the app, when you ask the app to fill your login or to update your attendance. Attendance, results, and notices are used only to display them to you in the app and its widget.

Sharing

Your credentials and portal data are not sold, shared, or sent to the developer or to any third party. The only network communication is between the app and the official IUST portal servers, and the services described below.

Third-party services

  • IUST portal (iust.ac.in and its student services) — the app signs in and reads pages there at your direction. The university's own policies apply to your portal account.
  • Google ML Kit — the app uses on-device text recognition to read the portal's login code (captcha) image. Recognition runs on your device; any limited operational or diagnostic data reported by ML Kit is governed by Google's privacy policy.

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics for tracking you.

Permissions

  • Internet — to reach the IUST portal.
  • Notifications — for attendance and portal-change alerts you enable.
  • Biometric / device credential — to unlock your saved login.
  • Foreground service and boot — to update attendance in the background at the interval you choose.

Security

Saved credentials are encrypted at rest as described above and are only decrypted on your device after you unlock them with your fingerprint or screen lock (PIN, pattern, or password).

Retention and deletion

Your saved login and locally stored portal data remain on your device until you remove them. You can delete the saved login at any time from within the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of its locally stored data.

Children

The app is intended for enrolled university students and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
